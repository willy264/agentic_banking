# agentic_banking

Research and product ideas for the [Airwallex Agentic Banking Hackathon](https://www.hackerearth.com/community/challenges/hackathon/agentic-banking-hackathon/).

The research covers all **12 official tracks**, with **36 developed ideas**, sponsor integration analysis, technical constraints, MVP scopes, and a recommended shortlist. Research was checked on **24 October 2026**; product access and event details may change.

## Current shortlist

- **CashProof:** connect reconciled receipts to executable treasury decisions.
- **Evidence Dividend:** purchase targeted evidence to justify releasing seller reserves.
- **CacheDividend:** share permitted upstream research costs and return savings to customers.
- **SecondLife Cart:** preserve unit-specific condition and approval in refurbished-product checkout.
- **DecisionBudget:** allocate paid inference to information that can change a business decision.
- **OneDebt:** prevent duplicate economic payments across reissued invoices and new request IDs.

These are proposed products, not implemented integrations. Example financial scenarios are synthetic. Detailed notes distinguish verified documentation from design assumptions and account-enablement dependencies.

Official brief graphics and public tab metadata are retained in `research/` for traceability. Downloaded website bundles, HTML, and full third-party documentation caches remain local and are excluded from version control; the reports link to their primary sources.
